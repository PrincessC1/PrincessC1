# Hi, I'm Princess Chishato

### Data with a human face

I work where two fields meet: **data analytics** and **human services**.

Before analytics, I spent five years in Zimbabwe supporting vulnerable women, children, and youth: survivors of gender-based violence, young women in shelters, and communities reached through public education on sexual harassment. Today I build data tools and AI systems, and I bring that frontline perspective to every dataset. Behind every row is a person.
